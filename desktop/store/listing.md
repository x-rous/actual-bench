## Product name

Actual Bench

## Description (max 10,000 characters)

Actual Bench turns your Actual Budget data into a complete picture of your financial life.

Connect it to your Actual Budget server, including the server built into the Actual desktop app on this PC, and see things about your money you didn't know. What you own and owe, what came in and went out, why your net worth changed, how long your cash would last, and when your loans will really be paid off. Everything is built from your own Actual data.

FINANCIAL INSIGHTS
One clear picture of your finances: net worth month by month, money in and out, income, spending, your budget, investments, property, loans and cards. Every figure opens the real transactions behind it, and a waterfall shows exactly what moved your net worth.

LOANS
Model your mortgage, car loan or personal loan the way your lender does: rate changes, extra payments, offsets, redraws and fees. Link a loan to your Actual accounts to compare the plan with your real payments, see the interest and time each extra payment saves, and follow your equity in the asset it paid for.

CREDIT CARDS
See what you owe, what is due and how much credit you have left across all your cards. Check statements against your transactions, follow payments and interest, and track instalment plans.

ASSETS
Bring your home, investments, vehicles and other assets into one view, using your Actual accounts as the ledger. Follow value, money invested, income, costs and returns over time, with shared ownership, depreciation and linked loans.

CASHFLOW PLANNING
Plan your cash month by month, years ahead, on top of what Actual recorded. See how low your cash gets, when, and whether you reach what you are saving for.

BUDGET COCKPIT
Twelve months of budget, spending and balances in one grid. Edit across months with spreadsheet-style selection, copy and paste, and fill.

AND MUCH MORE
• Accounts, categories, payees, tags, rules and schedules in one place, with search, bulk editing and CSV import and export
• Account classes that tell Bench what each account is: cash, savings, investment, property, loan or card
• Bank Reconciliation: compare CSV, OFX, QFX, QIF or PDF statements with your accounts and fix differences
• Cross Budget Sync between budget files, with currency conversion
• Backups of your budgets and Bench settings, on demand or on a schedule
• Automations: scheduled bank sync, backups and syncs
• Payee Cleanup and Rule Diagnostics to tidy up years of data
• ActualQL queries, Budget File Health checks and a Data Browser for the curious
• Progress cards you can share without showing any amounts

YOUR DATA STAYS YOURS
Actual Bench runs entirely on your computer and talks only to the Actual server you choose. There is no Actual Bench account, no cloud service, no analytics and no tracking.

WHAT YOU NEED
An Actual Budget server. The easiest is the one built into the Actual desktop app: choose Set up sync, then Start. Actual Bench finds it and connects in one click.

Actual Bench is an independent, open-source companion app for Actual Budget, made by a member of the community. It is not made by the Actual Budget project.

## Submission options: restricted capability (`runFullTrust`)

> Actual Bench is a desktop application built with Electron. It runs a local server process on the user's computer, bound to 127.0.0.1 only, and shows it in its own window. It needs full trust to start that process and to read and write its local database in the user's app data folder. It does not install services, drivers or startup tasks.

## Notes for certification (Submission options → Notes for certification)

> Actual Bench is a companion app for Actual Budget (open source, https://actualbudget.org). It needs an Actual Budget server to show any data. To test it:
>
> 1. Install the Actual desktop app from https://actualbudget.org/download/ and open it.
> 2. Choose "Create test file" to create a sample budget. Then choose "Set up sync" (bottom of the budget list), then "Start", and set any server password. Open the budget and register it with the server when Actual offers.
> 3. Open Actual Bench. Set an Actual Bench password (first start only).
> 4. On the connect screen, keep "Direct Actual Server" selected and click "Use http://localhost:5007". Enter the server password from step 2, click "Load budgets", select the budget and click "Connect".
> 5. Explore Overview, Financial Insights, Accounts, Budget Cockpit and the other pages in the sidebar.
>
> The app connects only to the server the user enters. It runs its own local server on 127.0.0.1 (port 47806) and has no account system.
