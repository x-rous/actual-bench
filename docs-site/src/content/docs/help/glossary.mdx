Plain definitions of the words you will meet in Actual Bench. Where a term has its own guide, it
links there.

## Actual and connections

- **Actual Budget** - the budgeting app Actual Bench works with. Your budget lives there.
- **Actual Server** - the server that holds your Actual budgets.
- **actual-http-api** - a separate server that gives other tools access to Actual. Bench can connect
  through it.
- **Direct mode** - connecting straight to your Actual Server, the way Actual's own app does. See
  [Connect to a budget](../../getting-started/connecting/).
- **HTTP API mode** - connecting through `actual-http-api`.
- **Encryption password** - the extra password on an end-to-end encrypted budget. It is not your
  server password.
- **Remember this budget** - saving a budget's server password, encrypted with your Actual Bench
  password, so you can reconnect in one click.
- **Enrolled budget** - a budget whose credentials are saved for scheduled work, so automations can
  open it with the app closed. See [Automations](../../user-guide/automations/#unattended-access).
- **Credential vault** / **vault key** - where enrolled credentials are kept, encrypted, and the key
  that opens them. The vault is **locked** when Bench cannot find the key that sealed them.

## Your budget

- **On-budget / off-budget account** - whether an account's money takes part in your budget.
- **Account class** - what kind of account it is (Checking, Savings, Credit Card, Property, Loan…).
  A label in Bench only. See [Accounts](../../user-guide/accounts/#account-classes).
- **Accessible** - whether you could use an account's money now (**Available now**) or not
  (**Locked**). Available money is cash; locked money only counts towards net worth.
- **Left out of reports** - an off-budget account that Financial Insights ignores completely.
- **Budget mode** - **Envelope** (give real money to categories) or **Tracking** (compare a plan with
  what happened), the two ways Actual budgets.
- **Transfer payee** - the payee Actual keeps for each account, used for transfers.
- **Rule**, **condition**, **action**, **stage** - a rule's conditions say when it applies, its
  actions say what it does, and its stage (`pre`, `default`, `post`) says when it runs.
- **Schedule** - a planned one-off or recurring transaction.
- **Tag** - a `#word` in a transaction's notes.

## Working in Bench

- **Draft** / **staged change** - an edit waiting in the **Changes** panel. It reaches Actual when
  you select **Save**. See [Work safely](../../getting-started/core-concepts/).
- **Save** / **Discard** - write the draft to Actual, or throw it away.
- **Preview** / **Apply** - see what a sync or reconciliation would change, then write the rows you
  chose.
- **Usage Inspector** - the **Info** drawer showing what depends on a record.
- **ActualQL** - Actual's JSON query language. See [ActualQL Queries](../../user-guide/actualql/).
- **Snapshot** - a fresh export of your budget file, opened read-only in your browser by Budget File
  Health and the Data Browser.
- **Metadata database** - Bench's own database under `/data`. It holds Bench's records, never a copy
  of your budget.
- **App Health** - the page where Bench checks its own storage, vault and workers.

## Money and reports

- **Net worth** - everything you own less everything you owe.
- **Money kept** - money received less money spent.
- **Savings rate** - money kept as a share of money received.
- **Cash buffer** - how many months your cash would cover a typical month.
- **In transit** - a transfer whose other side is missing, or falls in another period.
- **Investment activity** - uncategorized entries inside an investment account: price changes,
  dividends, fees.
- **Equity** - what an asset is worth less your share of the loan against it.
- **Loan-to-value (LTV)** - the loan against an asset as a share of its value.

## Loans, cards and assets

- **Tracked loan** - a loan linked to its account in Actual, so each repayment is matched and split.
  See [Loans](../../user-guide/assets-debt/).
- **Principal** / **interest** - the part of a repayment that lowers what you owe, and the part that
  pays the lender.
- **Statement** - one bank statement for a card: its balance, minimum and due date. See
  [Credit Cards](../../user-guide/credit-cards/).
- **Instalment plan** - a purchase the bank lets you repay in instalments.
- **Asset Ledger** - an asset's account in Actual, row by row, with a type for each entry. See
  [Assets](../../user-guide/asset-registry/).

## Reconciliation and sync

- **Session** - one reconciliation of one account against one statement, saved so you can come
  back to it. See [Bank Reconciliation](../../user-guide/bank-reconciliation/).
- **Statement layout** - how to read one bank's PDF table, saved for next month.
- **Imported payee** - the bank's own text for a transaction, kept beside your tidy payee name.
- **Flow** - a saved one-way sync from a source to a target. See
  [Cross Budget Sync](../../user-guide/budget-sync/).
- **Mapping** - Bench's record that a source item was copied to a target item, so it is never copied
  twice.
- **Safe item** - a row a sync can apply without review, such as a new transaction.
- **Review queue** - rows a sync could not prove safe, waiting for you.
- **Locked rate** - the exchange rate a synced transaction was copied with. It does not change unless
  you choose to update it.
- **Auto-pause** - an automation or flow pausing itself after several failed runs.
