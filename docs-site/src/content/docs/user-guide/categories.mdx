Categories shows every category group and category in one table, income and expense side by side.
Rename, move, hide and delete them in bulk, or bring a whole structure in from a spreadsheet.

Open it from **Budget Setup → Categories**. Changes are staged and go to Actual when you select
**Save**. It uses the shared [editing model](../../getting-started/editing-and-saving/).

![The Categories page with income and expense groups expanded, each category showing visibility and rule references.](../../../assets/screenshots/user-guide/categories.png)

*Groups and their categories in one list, with visibility and rule references on every row.*

## Groups and categories

- Create groups (income or expense) and the categories inside them.
- Rename anything inline, and collapse or expand groups one at a time or all together.
- To move a category, click its group cell and choose another group, or select several expense
  categories and choose **Move to group…**. Income categories stay in the income group.
- Add notes to categories and to groups. Notes save as soon as you confirm them.

## Hide and delete

- Hide or show a single category or a whole group.
- Deleting a category shows its transactions and rule references first. Deleting a group adds up
  every child category and its transactions, so you see the whole effect before anything is staged.
- When you delete saved categories, you can **Move transactions and budget amounts to** another
  category on the same side, as Actual does. For income categories Actual moves the transactions
  only. Leave it empty and the transactions become uncategorized.

## Filter and CSV

- Filter by name, type (income or expense) and visibility.
- **CSV columns:** `type` (`group` or `category`, required), `name` (required), `group`,
  `is_income`, `hidden`. Put each group's row above the categories that belong to it.

## Common problems

- **An income category will not move:** income categories belong to the income group.
- **Imported categories landed without a group:** check that each group row comes before its
  categories in the CSV.
