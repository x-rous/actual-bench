title: Credit Cards
description: Link Actual card accounts, review statements and track repayments and debt history.

Open **Tools → Credit Cards** after connecting a budget. Link each card to an existing on-budget Actual account. The workspace reads transactions; it does not change your financial ledger.

## Set up a card

In **Accounts**, classify the account as **Credit Card**. A group-inherited classification also qualifies. When no cards are registered, the centered **No credit cards yet** message includes an **Add card** button. Select the account; Bench uses its current Actual name throughout the workspace. There are no nickname, issuer or last-four-digit fields.

Set the statement closing day and payment due rule: days after closing, or a day of the month. Short months use their last calendar day. The minimum-payment estimate uses a percentage and a fixed floor, capped at the debt owed. Bank-confirmed statement values always override these estimates. Closing day is required during creation; the next two closing → due previews update as you enter the billing rules. A live timeline marks today, the upcoming closing dates and the payment window, with a due-date marker and days-to-pay count. It updates as you change the billing inputs using the same calendar rules. Invalid dates remain on the current step until corrected.

Card creation has two short steps: **Card & billing** selects the Actual account, tracking start, closing and due-date rules, appearance and network; **Classification & terms** sets fee/interest/cashback categories, optional credit limit and purchase APR, minimum percentage/floor and auto-pay. The collapsed **More details** chevron reveals status, annual fee, renewal/opened dates, interest illustration and time zone. Color swatches stay on one row with Network aligned to the right. A dashed + opens the custom color picker. The decorative card face uses the selected color, subtle shading and a chip, without printed text. Settings use a wider right-hand drawer with section shortcuts and a fixed Save/Cancel footer. Field info popovers explain behavior without long inline text. New cards default to **5%** with a **100** minimum floor; saved values are preserved. Status is **Active** or **Inactive**, which does not hide debt or stop history. Appearance/network do not affect calculations. Borrowing rates belong to the individual transaction’s plan, not the card’s settings.

**Interest category in Actual**, **Fees category in Actual** and **Cashback category in Actual** list the budget category directory, including unused categories. They identify existing postings, not calculate charges. Individual classifications override mappings; incoming transfer payments remain locked. Choose **Classify individually** to leave a mapping unset. Fees and interest must use different categories. Category-loading failures offer Retry and retain saved mappings.

The time zone defaults to your local zone and only determines today for statement closing and overdue status. Actual transaction dates remain unchanged; no intraday posting times are inferred.

The individual **Overview** keeps payment obligations beside current credit utilization. The payment panel shows the bank statement balance/minimum, confirmed amounts paid, full and minimum remaining, credits and pending amounts, due date, status and evidence. Review repayments and Correct bank facts stay beside it. The activity bridge explains current Actual debt versus the latest billed balance with spending, borrowing, payments, credits and unexplained differences. Card terms and repayment plans remain on Overview. Statement history is in **Debt & history** and repayment history in **Payments**, without duplicate panels on Overview. Review alerts appear once, with direct actions. The layout prioritizes desktop use; wide transaction tables scroll when needed. Statuses use text as well as color.

## Review automatically prepared statements

Bench loads all history from each card’s **First tracked statement** and prepares closed billing cycles from Actual. There is no global month-range selector or 36-month limit. Until a tracking start is chosen or an opening statement is saved, six months of recent activity is loaded to help with initial setup. Statement balances are reconstructed from Actual's signed account balance and subsequent activity, including future-dated entries. These are estimates until you choose **Confirm bank values**. Dates and amounts remain editable for bank exceptions. Use the official bank due date, including holiday adjustments, when confirming or correcting a statement. Empty zero-balance drafts before the first known activity or statement are omitted, except the explicitly selected **First tracked statement**: that cycle remains available even without transactions, including a bank closing-date exception. Its brought-forward value stays blank until entered; no earlier transactions never implies a confirmed zero. An inferred nonzero opening balance is retained. The date opened, when supplied, bounds automatic drafts.

Confirm your latest bank statement first to establish an opening anchor. Earlier statements can be viewed or entered incrementally. Their repayment outcomes use the loaded transfer dates independently of the opening debt anchor. You do not need to reconstruct the account from day one.

For the first tracked statement, **Balance brought forward (optional)** accepts the previous statement balance before this cycle’s activity. Enter zero if nothing was owed, or a negative amount for card credit; leave it blank if unknown. Bench calculates **balance brought forward + included charges − included payments/credits**, compares it with the first statement’s bank balance, and enables **Save & verify** when variance is zero and all evidence/decisions are complete. No earlier tracked statement is created. The brought-forward value does not independently verify earlier transactions, and the first statement’s closing balance remains the repayment anchor, so debt is not counted twice. You can enter or clear this value later through **Correct bank facts**. Without it, the review explicitly says the opening balance was not supplied and supports transaction confirmation only.

Saving bank values opens transaction review without automatically verifying. Review begins with **All** selected. Its equation strip shows starting balance, included charges and credits, calculated balance and variance; the bank balance and evidence status remain visible. Payee, notes, category and classification each have a separate single-line column. Search, decision counts, clearing status and **Unclassified** filtering remain available. Include, Exclude and Undecided form a compact segmented icon control with accessible labels; undecided rows have light amber backgrounds. Selected rows support bulk decisions, including selected rows hidden by filters, and bulk classification. Payments and linked plan postings cannot be reclassified in bulk. One neutral banner summarizes genuinely unclassified activity instead of repeating error labels on every row. Selection and pending decisions survive filtering. Row menus expose moving statement membership and plan actions. Ownership-locked rows show a lock and their owning-statement reason, preventing inclusion in two statements.

Add a notes tag such as `#2026-09` in Actual to identify the statement that **closes in September 2026**, regardless of the transaction's Actual date. All transfers ignore these tags, including outgoing transfers. One valid month tag on other transactions suggests membership and adjusts automatic draft balances across closing cutoffs. Drafts reconstruct the outstanding billing balance cumulatively rather than summing only that month’s purchases. Untagged or conflicting-tag activity retains its date-based estimate; conflicting month tags need review. Bank-confirmed values always take priority. Manual decisions take precedence. Tags describe membership in the bank statement, rather than which bill a later repayment settles. Changed non-transfer source notes invalidate saved evidence and require review.

Statement review uses Actual transaction dates and does not offer a bank posting-date editor. Repayment windows and lateness use the Actual card-side transfer date. Clicking a membership decision records a default reason that you can edit.

**Confirm transactions** saves membership when the opening balance is unknown without claiming reconciliation. With a known starting balance and complete evidence, **Save & verify** saves pending decisions and verifies in one action. Otherwise **Save decisions** preserves the draft and actionable blockers explain what remains. An unchanged verified statement shows **Verified**. These actions save Bench metadata; global **Save** applies staged Actual edits. The Statements list combines confirmed records and automatic drafts newest first. Its compact columns show statement date/period, statement balance, bank minimum, due date, payment progress and evidence status. Expand a row to inspect its balance movement, detailed payment amounts and dates, bank editing, transaction review, reset or deletion. An unconfirmed or incomplete estimate never presents a provisional difference as a confirmed bank mismatch.

Verification needs confirmed bank facts, a continuous preceding bank statement, sufficient transaction coverage, no unresolved cutoff decisions or duplicate membership, and zero balance variance. Matching totals alone are insufficient. The opening anchor cannot establish its own earlier history. Source changes reopen affected evidence; unrelated later transactions do not invalidate an already reviewed statement. Another statement claiming or releasing a boundary transaction that this statement already excluded does not mark its source changed. Genuine included-transaction conflicts and moves still require review. The header shows temporary action feedback for six seconds beside a persistent statement-health control, which names how many statements need review. On All cards, these sit beside the page title; on an individual card, they sit beside the tabs on one line. Select the health control for verified, needs-review, source-changed and unavailable-evidence counts. A successful action does not certify every older statement.

Use **Correct bank facts** to update the current saved statement without a correction note or correction log. Session Undo and stale-editor revision checks remain available. Dependent later statements return to review. The Actual read timestamp and activity-loading status appear in the top-right toolbar beside the workspace actions. Refresh syncs the Direct budget first, then reloads balances, classifications and transactions. HTTP uses the existing server-read transport; it does not expose a separate sync action. A failed sync or refresh retains saved records and open drafts, keeps affected results unavailable and offers retry with Refresh.

## Track repayments

Statements and Payments have search, filters, sortable headers and visible primary actions. Statements summarize tracking, settlement, verification and observed interest above flat rows. Payments summarize recorded transfers, pending clearance, matches and unmatched transfers. Their single incoming-transfer table shows date, funding account/payee, notes, amount, clearing and applied statement. The arrow opens read-only payment details with From/To accounts, both source transactions and complete notes. Missing funding evidence does not prevent recognizing the card-side transfer. Statement links open and locate the relevant statement row; payment details never offer synthetic payment creation.

A repayment is a positive incoming transfer on the card account. Both cleared and uncleared incoming transfers count toward recorded payment totals. Uncleared amounts are shown as pending; they are not reported as cleared bank payments. Funding-side details enrich the display but are not required to recognize a repayment. Notes tags, spending classification, membership review and the opening debt anchor do not decide which statement receives it.

Each statement's repayment window runs **after its closing date through the next closing date, inclusive**. A transfer on the next closing date belongs to the preceding statement; one the following day belongs to the new statement. Recurring closing rules bound a missing cycle, so a missing statement cannot collect later cycles' payments. Each transfer belongs to one window. An official statement due date always overrides the card’s recurring due rule, including holiday changes to the 2nd or 3rd. Its repayment window extends through that due date if needed. Overlapping windows assign each transfer once, prioritizing a bank-confirmed statement whose explicit due date matches the payment date.

Compact statement rows combine a payment outcome badge, due-relative timing and concise supporting amounts. Confirmed incoming transfers establish payment satisfaction, while pending transfers remain explicit. Full settlement suppresses routine minimum-success information. Partial payments expose the minimum shortfall independently of full remaining; a met minimum after the deadline leaves **Balance carried**. Full amounts recorded but uncleared remain **Awaiting clearance**. Credits can settle the full balance, but never satisfy a positive bank minimum. Zero/credit statements identify no required payment or card credit; unavailable evidence never becomes a zero payment. The Minimum column retains the original bank fact.

Statement badges, status filters, sorting and review counts share the same evidence state. **Balance mismatch**, **Ready to verify**, **Verified**, **Source changed**, missing anchors and unavailable data stay distinct. The header health control opens attention-filtered statements, with estimates counted separately. **Clear filters** restores all rows. Expanding a row keeps bank editing and review beside the detail; its action menu provides reset/delete. Payment and verification actions use the existing guarded review workflow.

Expanded movement uses **Brought forward** for the first cycle and **Previous balance** thereafter. It lists purchases/borrowing, interest, fees, payments in the billing period, refunds, cashback, other adjustments and calculated balance. Billing-period credits are distinct from post-closing settlement payments. Only a confirmed, complete nonzero reconciliation difference is shown as a bank mismatch; unresolved membership decisions or missing transaction evidence remain review blockers. Classification does not invalidate a reconciled statement; unclassified charges appear as a separate grey amount and do not prevent arithmetic reconciliation. Valid credits are not errors.

You do not need to allocate a repayment to individual purchases. Statement matching is automatic. Bench retains an internal opening-debt breakdown for analysis, separate from repayment tracking. Payments beyond a statement obligation are shown separately from remaining card credit. They may have funded later activity, so only the signed Actual account surplus establishes current credit. Pending payments remain explicit. Rolling statement balances are never added together as new principal.

## Understand debt and history

The **All cards** table shows each card’s remaining obligation, payment status, net activity since its latest confirmed bank statement, utilization, available credit and current debt. Sort by **Next due**, **Largest debt** or **Name**. Footer totals disclose missing coverage, minimum and pending amounts, card credit and observed interest with its reporting period. Purchase APR remains visible with card details and in Settings. Debt and interest trends remain below the table.

**Debt & history** focuses on actual history: debt carried at each due date, billed balance trends with growth drivers in tooltips, and cumulative identified interest with fees separately reported. Actual carried debt appears only after the due date has passed, uses confirmed payments by that date and stays unchanged by later repayment. Upcoming statements are omitted. Missing coverage and pending deadline evidence remain unknown rather than zero. Historical charts connect consecutive known statement observations with straight lines and retain observation markers. These lines show a trend, not reconstructed daily balances; missing periods interrupt the line. Filled markers identify confirmed bank values or observed Actual evidence; hollow markers identify totals including estimates. Source labels in tooltips explain which evidence supports each point. Late-payment outcomes remain separate. A known-zero interest chart is collapsed until requested; missing classification is explained without claiming zero charges. A known zero carried balance uses a compact positive empty state. Empty repayment plans show an Add plan action without table headers or an expected-entry legend.

Balance and cumulative-interest trends reuse the loan-history line chart; monthly interest charges use bars. All charts use visible value axes, with theme colors, dashed grids, exact-value tooltips and keyboard point navigation. Focus a chart and use the arrow keys to inspect statements. A single known observation is shown as a labelled point. Charts respect reduced motion. **View trend values** opens a dialog; **Review spending, refunds and interest classification** shows the source table using a labelled button.

Select the card's Actual interest/fee categories in Settings to identify those charges automatically, or classify individual rows with a reason. Without category mappings or complete current individual classification, interest, fees and new-spending breakdowns remain unavailable rather than claiming zero charges or treating all outflows as spending. **Set charge categories** opens settings directly. Portfolio totals disclose how many cards have classification coverage. Merchant names are not proof of an interest charge. **Interest charged** uses Actual posting dates and shows the available reporting period. It includes only loaded activity within that period, including history crossing a calendar year; it does not fetch earlier transactions to manufacture an annual total. Interest charged is not claimed to be interest paid, and transfers alone cannot establish the bank's interest/principal allocation or lifetime totals.

**Interest illustration** defaults to **Daily simple Actual/365** for new cards and uses the same engine as Loans & Debt. Supported daily simple 365/360-day profiles assume purchase APR on all current debt, monthly capitalization, no grace period, no new spending and no repayments for 30 days. The first capitalization occurs at the next statement closing date after the illustration starts; interest then accrues on the increased balance. Unsupported combinations show an unavailable result. Bank-posted charges stay separate; Bench never posts the estimate into Actual. Choose Off when these assumptions do not fit the card; an existing explicit Off setting is preserved.

Saved actions show feedback on the right of the tabs, below Settings. **Undo** reverses recent workspace changes; close an editor or review first. A source refresh preserves unsaved editors and review decisions.

### Balance movement and minimum-payment cost

All workbench dates use **DD MMM YYYY**, including statement periods, payment details, date inputs, chart axes/tooltips and payoff dates. Search accepts the displayed format as well as ISO dates; sorting and calculations still use stored ISO dates. Source transaction notes and `#YYYY-MM` tags retain their original text.

The compact statement table shows closing date/period, statement balance, bank minimum, due date, payment outcome, evidence status and direct actions. Statement amounts align left beneath their headers. The one row action is **Confirm bank values** for estimates, **Review transactions** for saved statements needing review, or **Open** for currently verified statements. Open goes to transaction review without expanding a row. The row menu contains **Edit bank values**, reset and deletion. Verified statements use a quiet check; chips emphasize exceptions. Upcoming payments more than seven days away are blue with a countdown; due soon/today are amber. A missed minimum is red. Full payment suppresses routine minimum-success repetition while its historical threshold remains recorded.

**Fully paid** filters for confirmed payments covering the statement balance; **Balance covered by credits** is separate. Credits reduce full remaining but cannot satisfy a positive minimum. Pending transfers/credits cannot establish confirmed settlement. Original statement minimums remain bank facts, independent of amounts remaining. Zero and credit-balance statements show no required payment or card credit.

Review uses separate payee, notes, category and classification columns. Long text remains searchable and can be read in a popover. Include, Exclude and Undecided are icon buttons with visible selection and accessible labels; undecided rows are light amber. Decision counts appear once in the filter buttons. The evidence/save explanation is available from the ⓘ beside the reconciliation result; blockers remain visible. **Save decisions** appears when verification is blocked; **Save & verify** commits eligible decisions together. Without a known opening balance, **Confirm transactions** confirms membership only. Classification changes save immediately to Bench session metadata; membership decisions remain draft until the statement action. Closing a changed editor or review asks before discarding unsaved work. No classification or review action changes Actual transactions.

Debt history aligns carried balance, billed balance and cumulative interest in three equal-height desktop panels, with a compact summary strip above. Balance tooltips identify spending, interest, fees, refunds and cycle payments separately. The cumulative-interest chart can show a hypothetical zero-interest baseline; this is not a reconstruction of a past grace period or interest paid. Upcoming obligations use **Upcoming**, **Due soon** (within seven days), **Due today** or **Overdue**, with payment outcomes kept separate.

The collapsed **What if I only pay the minimum?** section opens optional **Cost of minimum payments** modelling. It uses the Loans & Debt engine from current Actual debt. It keeps the configured minimum percentage/floor and APR unchanged, with no new spending or fees, daily simple accrual and monthly capitalization. Payments start at the next projected cycle due date; every later due date is independently recalculated from its billing cycle, preserving the configured day after February and other short months. Bank-confirmed exceptions apply to saved statements, not hypothetical future cycles. Interest is not capped by the credit limit. It shows projected balance including accrued interest, cumulative future interest and a payoff date only after both posted debt and exact accrued interest are settled under the engine’s rounding policy. Small residual interest may require another posting/payment cycle. Chart tooltips distinguish posted from accrued interest; summaries and charts end on the same payoff date or 30-year horizon. Warnings distinguish minimum payments that do not cover accrued interest from debt repayment too slow to finish within the horizon. Otherwise it reports **Not repaid within 30 years** and interest over that bounded horizon, rather than a lifetime total. An unavailable configuration or unsupported engine outcome is reported explicitly. **Future interest if paid in full now** is a hypothetical zero-future-interest benchmark, with the actual payoff amount stated separately; accrued/trailing charges and issuer grace-period rules are not inferred. Separate purchase, cash-advance and balance-transfer APR buckets, loss/restoration of grace and issuer-specific residual-interest rules are not modeled. Monthly capitalization remains an explicit illustration assumption, not a claim about your issuer. These scenarios never change Actual transactions or recorded bank balances.

A transaction saved as included in one statement appears automatically excluded and locked in other statement reviews, with a reason such as **Included in statement 30 Sep 2026**. Bulk decisions cannot include it elsewhere. To reassign it, open its owning review and choose **Move** or **Move selected**. Select a confirmed destination and stage the move. Saving excludes it from the source and includes it in the destination atomically; the destination returns to review. Closing without saving discards moves. Stale revisions, changed source evidence, incomplete coverage and other-card targets are rejected. You can also exclude and save before including elsewhere. Changing source transaction details does not release this ownership automatically.


## Net activity since statement

Overview’s **Current balance and activity** shows the equation latest statement + charges − payments/credits + unexplained difference = current balance, with a breakdown bar and detailed activity groups. Genuine unclassified charges/credits stay separate from Other. Uncleared activity and incomplete bridges use an amber investigation panel. Open **View contributing transactions** to inspect the entries. Scheduled future transactions are excluded from both current workspace debt/credit metrics and this bridge; Actual remains authoritative. Available credit includes a positive account surplus consistently across portfolio and individual views. The difference is not automatically called new purchases.


## Choose the first tracked statement

In card Settings, set **First tracked statement** to the closing date of the first cycle you want Bench to track. Leaving it blank initially uses the first saved statement. That specific confirmed statement establishes the opening anchor; earlier cycles are not automatically drafted or saved. Each card has its own boundary. Existing opening statements supply the boundary for older session records.

The first tracked statement controls both history display and activity loading for that card. All tracked cycles through today load automatically, so a start nine months ago shows all nine months; longer histories are not capped at 36 months. Each card loads its own range on the All cards dashboard. Reads begin at the first cycle’s start with a two-day margin for posting cutoffs, without adding earlier tracked cycles or moving the anchor. Exact bank-entered period dates override the recurring period estimate for the first statement. Missing or failed activity reads remain explicit; they are never treated as zero balances or charges.

Changing the start reopens saved statements for review and recalculates the opening anchor. Move it earlier to record an earlier opening cycle; Bench does not invent its bank balance. Moving it forward requires deleting saved statements before the new start first, so changing Settings cannot silently discard bank facts. If no saved confirmed statement matches the chosen date, the opening anchor remains missing.

## Reset or delete a saved statement

Expand the statement row and open **Statement actions**. Reset and delete require confirmation and support workspace **Undo**.

- **Reset transaction review** removes the statement’s saved inclusion/exclusion/review decisions and bank-posting overrides, clears verification, and regenerates suggestions next time you review. It releases transaction ownership while preserving bank values and separately saved activity classifications. Dependent later statements and saved reviews referencing released transactions reopen for review.
- **Delete saved statement** removes its saved bank values, review decisions and allocations referencing its opening origin. It releases transaction ownership and reopens dependent statements. Actual Budget transactions and activity classifications are untouched. An unsaved automatic estimate may reappear; deletion removes the saved record, not the underlying billing cycle.

Deleting the opening statement preserves the configured tracking date. Later statements are not silently promoted to an opening anchor: restore the missing statement, use Undo, or explicitly change the tracking start in Settings. Revision guards reject actions from stale dialogs, and Undo restores the records and decisions without making older editor revisions valid again.


## All cards dashboard

The dashboard supports one card or many. Monthly statement debt uses month labels rather than inventing a closing date on the first of each month. Tooltips identify each contributing card, its actual closing date and balance; a month missing any registered card remains a gap. Confirmed values and estimates stay distinguished.

**Interest charged by month** appears whenever loaded Actual activity contains identified, cleared interest, even for a single reporting month. It displays identified monthly charges across the available card histories. Different tracking windows, unavailable cards and incomplete classifications do not hide known charges: tooltips label partial coverage, show each card’s exact reporting dates, and leave unknown contributions unavailable. Partial totals include identified charges only, not an estimate of missing interest. Months without any usable evidence remain gaps. The chart is hidden when no interest charges have been identified, leaving the debt chart the available width. The top Interest charged KPI retains each card’s available reporting period and coverage.

Payment status shows amount outcomes separately from timing: Upcoming, Due soon (within seven days), Due today, the number of days past the full-payment deadline, or the historical days late after settlement. A minimum met remains a minimum met even when the full balance is carried. The payment KPI shows nonzero minimum, overdue-minimum and pending amounts, flags partial coverage, and otherwise reports no minimum outstanding. Hover its amount for the confirmed-card count. Currency display is unchanged.

The generic explanation of chart lines, gaps and markers is omitted beneath charts. Source details remain available in the interactive tooltips.


## Cashback credits

Select **Cashback category in Actual** to identify positive non-transfer rewards automatically, or classify them individually as **Cashback / reward**. Ordinary non-transfer credits default to **Refund** unless a cashback mapping, reviewed choice or plan role applies. Incoming transfers remain **Payment**.

Cashback is a card credit, not a payment or refund. It is shown separately in statement balance movements, the current activity bridge and Debt & history, and does not appear in the **Payments** tab or inflate confirmed repayment totals. Actual Budget remains authoritative; these controls never create or edit a transaction.

Cashback received during a billing period is included once in that period’s balance reconciliation. After closing, cashback uses the existing statement date window to reduce the full amount remaining; the settlement date can therefore reflect both payments and cashback. Rows show **Settled** when cashback contributes, rather than claiming the entire balance was paid. Cleared cashback reduces confirmed remaining debt; pending cashback is disclosed separately and does not establish confirmed settlement or historical carried debt.

Cashback, refunds and other statement credits reduce full remaining but **do not satisfy the minimum payment**. Minimum satisfaction and its historical date use Actual payment transfers only. The UI explicitly says that credits reduce the balance but do not count toward the minimum; it can show a balance covered by credits alongside an unpaid minimum. Actual account credit remains separate from an excess payment against one statement.

## Classify transactions

Ordinary non-transfer outflows default to **Purchase**, and ordinary non-transfer inflows default to **Refund**. Both remain editable. Explicit classifications, plan posting roles and fee/interest/cashback category mappings take priority. Outgoing transfers and transactions without reliable transfer information remain **Unclassified**, with a neutral count/filter; it is not a classification you pick. **Other charge** and **Other statement credit** are explicit choices. Bench enforces directions:

| Charges (negative card amount) | Credits (positive card amount) |
| --- | --- |
| Purchase, Cash advance, Money transfer, Balance transfer in, Interest, Fee, Other charge | Payment, Refund, Cashback / reward, Other statement credit |

**Payment** is reserved for incoming Actual transfers and locked. Outgoing transfers are borrowing, never payment cancellations; classify them as Cash advance, Money transfer, Balance transfer in or Other charge. **Fee type** distinguishes annual, late, over-limit, cash-advance, foreign exchange, plan processing, plan monthly, VAT on fee and other. VAT stays a fee. Classification does not edit Actual categories, dates or amounts.

Arithmetic does not require classification: genuinely unclassified charges appear as **Charges (unclassified)** in movement details and grey chart segments, and remain included in charge totals. Missing classification never makes a known billed balance dashed or unavailable. Interest reporting still distinguishes identified charges from complete classification coverage; a default purchase does not prove that all bank interest was identified. Bulk classification applies selected transactions atomically with one Undo. Membership decisions and economic classification are separate: Include says which statement owns the transaction, not what it represents.

## Repayment plans and instalments

Attach a plan to an existing classified purchase, cash advance, money transfer or balance transfer. Use the transaction row menu, the classification editor’s **Add an instalment plan next**, or Overview’s **Add plan**. A purchase can be partially converted; the amount cannot exceed its Actual charge. One original transaction has one editable plan. No debt or Actual posting is created.

The editor records converted amount, start/first due date, number of instalments, monthly amount or minimum rule, event-specific APR and upfront/monthly fees. **Does the monthly amount already include interest and fees?** distinguishes inclusive and separate costs. Principal + estimated interest + upfront fee + monthly fees reconcile to expected total. Upfront fees are separate on the first due date. The first due date must be after the start date; included monthly fees must be smaller than the instalment.

For inclusive **Fixed instalments**, monthly amount × term determines the total contract cost. Principal and included monthly fees are separated from the remaining finance cost; recorded APR does not add interest again. Only minor rounding can change the last inclusive instalment, and insufficient monthly totals must be corrected before saving. Fixed principal instalments with separately billed costs and **Minimum + deadline** use the shared daily-simple Actual/365 calculation and percentage/floor primitive. A materially different final fixed amount requires explicit review/acceptance. The final minimum/deadline entry clears the expected remainder. Unpaid billed interest may capitalize in this illustration; these are not issuer-confirmed calculations, grace periods or multiple APR buckets. **Promotional rate**, **Deferred interest** and **Ordinary revolving** record terms/expiry/conditions but show **Not calculated**.

Link existing Actual postings by role:

- **Conversion credit**: the bank credit offsetting an original purchase, not a refund or payment.
- **Instalment billed**: an actual billed charge linked to its instalment number.
- **Plan fee**: a real fee posting, separately linked.
- **Refund linked to original purchase**: a refund that asks for plan review, without silently cancelling it.

Each posting belongs to one plan; incoming payment transfers cannot be relabelled as plan postings. A billed instalment has one linked posting, with separate fees linked as Plan fee. Linked entries retain their actual amount and statement membership. Observed billed charges/fees are shown separately from expected cost, never added together. The original charge and any conversion credit remain in the Actual ledger once; terms do not duplicate them.

**Expected** instalments use dashed/outlined presentation. **Billed** means a linked Actual charge exists, not that it was paid. Incoming Actual payments alone supply “paid” amounts. The plan table shows amount, billed progress, next expected entry, still-expected schedule total and status; “still expected” is not an independently reconstructed Actual debt balance.

Plans start **Active**. Completed, Cancelled or reactivated status requires an explicit user save and never changes silently. A linked refund, missing past expected instalment or changed source produces **Needs review**, without changing the saved lifecycle. When the original charge changed, accept its current evidence explicitly before saving; reclassification may be required. Stale links must be reviewed and relinked. Past expected zero amounts are not flagged as missing postings. Review alerts remain visible even on completed/cancelled plans. A plan whose original source disappeared can be removed with confirmation; this removes only its Bench metadata/links and supports Undo. Revision checks preserve concurrent drafts. Plans and other workspace metadata remain tab-session memory, not durable app-DB records.

Statement periods sit beneath the full closing date and omit repeated years (for example **01 Sep – 30 Sep**); cross-year periods retain the starting year. Row actions align right. Balance, Interest & fees and Minimum use equal compact widths; Outcome keeps its supporting text beside the badge without repeating the minimum-due amount. The Balance header is shortened, all amounts align left, and payment timing takes the remaining width after fixed compact columns. **New charges** totals included purchases, borrowing and other outflows excluding interest and fees. **Credits** totals included refunds, cashback and all other positive statement entries except payments. Both totals follow statement transaction membership and remain unavailable when coverage or membership is unresolved. **Carried at due** is the confirmed balance remaining at the deadline, unaffected by later payments. It is not today’s remaining amount and rolling statement balances must not be added together as new debt. **Carried at due** has its own left-aligned column. **Interest & fees** shows their combined amount in its own column, with red nonzero costs and a popover separating the two; unavailable components keep the total unknown rather than zero. Timing explicitly identifies a minimum-payment threshold when the full balance remains unpaid, without repeating an elapsed overdue-day count on every old row.


### Payment effect

Payments describes each recorded transfer’s effect on its matched bank statement, rather than guessing intent: **Minimum payment**, **Partial payment**, **Completes statement** or **Excess payment**. Partial payments also indicate whether the minimum is covered or still short. Earlier cleared transfers and credits are considered through that date; later or pending activity cannot retroactively establish an earlier payment effect. Credits reduce the full balance but never satisfy the minimum. Excess over a statement obligation does not necessarily mean unused card credit.

Multiple cleared payments on one date show **Same-day payments**; a payment with same-day credits shows **Payment + credits**. Their combined effect is explained without claiming an intraday order. Pending, unknown-clearing, unmatched, estimated-statement, future-dated and incomplete-history cases remain explicit. These are derived labels only: Actual transactions, statement matching, allocations and saved payment outcomes are unchanged.
