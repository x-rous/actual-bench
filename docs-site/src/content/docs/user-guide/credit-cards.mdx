---
title: Credit Cards
description: Link Actual card accounts, review statements and track repayments and debt history.
---

Open **Tools → Credit Cards** after connecting a budget. Link each card to an existing on-budget Actual account. The workspace reads transactions; it does not change your financial ledger.

## Set up a card

In **Accounts**, classify the account as **Credit Card**. A group-inherited classification also qualifies. When no cards are registered, the centered **No credit cards yet** message includes an **Add card** button. Select the account and enter a nickname.

Set the statement closing day and payment due rule: days after closing, or a day of the month. Short months use their last calendar day. The minimum-payment estimate uses a percentage and a fixed floor, capped at the debt owed. Bank-confirmed statement values always override these estimates. If you leave the closing day blank, the first bank statement supplies the recurring closing day and days-to-due defaults.

The wider card dialog uses three fields per row. Extra fields are opened with a labelled button; the workspace does not use expandable arrows. Credit limit and purchase APR are optional. **More card details** holds other APRs, autopay, fees and renewal, opening date, card status and an optional time-zone override. Only the last four digits are needed; never enter a full card number.

**Interest category in Actual** and **Fees category in Actual** list the complete budget category directory, including categories without card transactions. The two mappings are always visible in card settings. These mappings identify posted charges; they do not calculate interest or fees. Choose **Classify individually** to leave a mapping unset. Category loading failures offer Retry and retain existing mappings.

The time zone defaults to your local zone and only determines today for statement closing and overdue status. Actual transaction dates remain unchanged; no intraday posting times are inferred.

Dialog fields use the app’s standard input size; transaction-table editors use its compact size. Panels, buttons and tabs use the app’s standard neutral styling; green marks settled payments, amber asks for attention and red marks overdue minimum payments; amber also retains late-payment outcomes and pending clearance. Statuses always include text. Overview groups information into **Statement and payment**, **Current balance and activity**, and **Card terms**, without separate boxes for every metric. The payment fields fit an adaptive row, and balance/activity sits beside compact card terms on desktop; narrow screens stack the sections. Bank balance and minimum remain distinct from confirmed payment shortfalls; pending clearance stays explicit. Correct bank facts, review repayments, inspect contributing transactions and edit terms directly from the related section. Payment warnings and actions appear once; the attention list contains unresolved statement issues. The activity breakdown shows a separate transaction total when it differs from the balance change or coverage is incomplete. Trend charts distinguish billed debt, debt carried at the deadline and observed charges.

## Review automatically prepared statements

Bench loads six months initially and prepares closed billing cycles from Actual. **Activity history window** expands this to 12, 24 or 36 months. Statement balances are reconstructed from Actual's signed account balance and subsequent activity, including future-dated entries. These are estimates until you choose **Confirm bank values**. Dates and amounts remain editable for bank exceptions. Use the official bank due date, including holiday adjustments, when confirming or correcting a statement. Empty zero-balance drafts before the first known activity or statement are omitted; an inferred nonzero opening balance is retained. The date opened, when supplied, bounds automatic drafts.

Confirm your latest bank statement first to establish an opening anchor. Earlier statements can be viewed or entered incrementally. Their repayment outcomes use the loaded transfer dates independently of the opening debt anchor. You do not need to reconstruct the account from day one.

Saving confirmed or corrected bank values opens transaction review directly; it does not automatically verify the statement. **Review transactions** also opens a full-page drill-down with Date, Payee, Notes, Amount and Cleared columns. Bank balance, calculated balance, variance and unresolved decisions sit in summary boxes above the table. Search transaction details, filter by decision or clearing status, and sort any column; **Needs review**, **Included**, **Excluded** and **All** show counts. Review always opens with **All** selected; use the counted views to focus on exceptions when needed. The applicable save action stays visible while the table scrolls. Select individual rows or all visible rows to include, exclude or request review in bulk. Selection survives filtering, and bulk actions include selected hidden rows; **Clear selection** resets it. Edits remain in the review draft until saved. Each row has three small icon-only buttons: check for **Include**, minus for **Exclude**, and question mark for **Undecided**. Only one is selected. Undecided rows have a very light amber background until resolved. Button titles and accessible labels explain the icons. Notes appear directly in the gray-headed review table, without expanding a row. Decisions stay in the draft until saved, and filtering never drops hidden decisions.

Add a notes tag such as `#2026-09` in Actual to identify the statement that **closes in September 2026**, regardless of the transaction's Actual date. All transfers ignore these tags, including outgoing transfers. One valid month tag on other transactions suggests membership and adjusts automatic draft balances across closing cutoffs. Drafts reconstruct the outstanding billing balance cumulatively rather than summing only that month’s purchases. Untagged or conflicting-tag activity retains its date-based estimate; conflicting month tags need review. Bank-confirmed values always take priority. Manual decisions take precedence. Tags describe membership in the bank statement, rather than which bill a later repayment settles. Changed non-transfer source notes invalidate saved evidence and require review.

Enable **Bank posting dates** only when needed. The bank may book a purchase on a different date near the closing cutoff, or receive a repayment on a different date from Actual's record. An optional posting-date correction keeps billing-membership evidence without changing Actual. Repayment windows and lateness use the Actual card-side transfer date. Clicking a membership decision records a default reason that you can edit.

**Confirm transactions** completes membership review for an opening statement or a gap without marking its balance reconciled. Once a continuous preceding statement and complete matching evidence are available, **Save & verify** saves pending decisions and verifies the balance in one action. When blocked, **Save decisions** saves the draft and specific messages explain what remains. An unchanged verified statement shows **Verified**. These actions save Bench metadata; the global **Save** applies staged Actual edits. A short inline status explains any remaining blocker. The statement list always combines confirmed records and automatic drafts in newest-period-first order, with an explicit **Statement date** (the closing date), total paid, full remaining, paid-in-full date, payment timing and minimum-payment status.

Verification needs confirmed bank facts, a continuous preceding bank statement, sufficient transaction coverage, no unresolved cutoff decisions or duplicate membership, and zero balance variance. Matching totals alone are insufficient. The opening anchor cannot establish its own earlier history. Source changes reopen affected evidence; unrelated later transactions do not invalidate an already reviewed statement. Another statement claiming or releasing a boundary transaction that this statement already excluded does not mark its source changed. Genuine included-transaction conflicts and moves still require review. The header shows temporary action feedback for six seconds beside a persistent statement-health control, which names how many statements need review. On All cards, these sit beside the page title; on an individual card, they sit beside the tabs on one line. Select the health control for verified, needs-review, source-changed and unavailable-evidence counts. A successful action does not certify every older statement.

Use **Correct bank facts** with a reason to retain a before/after correction trail. Dependent later statements return to review. The Actual read timestamp and activity-loading status appear in the top-right toolbar before the history-window selector. Refresh syncs the Direct budget first, then reloads balances, classifications and transactions. HTTP uses the existing server-read transport; it does not expose a separate sync action. A failed sync or refresh retains saved records and open drafts, keeps affected results unavailable and offers retry with Refresh.

## Track repayments

Statements and Payments use full-height tables with fixed top toolbars, search, filters, sortable headers and primary toolbar actions on the right; statement row actions are outlined. Statements can filter bank confirmation/review and repayment outcomes; Payments can filter clearing and statement matches. **Clear filters** resets search and filters. The Payments tab has one table of incoming transfers, newest first: date, paid-from account/payee, directly visible notes, amount, clearing status and the automatically matched statement. **View details** opens both sides of the transfer, including full notes, without any allocation inputs. A missing funding transaction does not prevent payment recognition. Click the statement date or **View statement payment status** to open Statements. Card balance metrics appear only on Overview; the card title and tabs stay at the top.

A repayment is a positive incoming transfer on the card account. Both cleared and uncleared incoming transfers count toward recorded payment totals. Uncleared amounts are shown as pending; they are not reported as cleared bank payments. Funding-side details enrich the display but are not required to recognize a repayment. Notes tags, spending classification, membership review and the opening debt anchor do not decide which statement receives it.

Each statement's repayment window runs **after its closing date through the next closing date, inclusive**. A transfer on the next closing date belongs to the preceding statement; one the following day belongs to the new statement. Recurring closing rules bound a missing cycle, so a missing statement cannot collect later cycles' payments. Each transfer belongs to one window. An official statement due date always overrides the card’s recurring due rule, including holiday changes to the 2nd or 3rd. Its repayment window extends through that due date if needed. Overlapping windows assign each transfer once, prioritizing a bank-confirmed statement whose explicit due date matches the payment date.

The statement list separates **Payment progress** from **Payment timing**, including distinct minimum and full-payment outcomes. Total paid sums recorded incoming transfers in that window, including uncleared transfers and overpayment, with the pending amount shown separately. The paid date is when cumulative recorded transfers first cover the confirmed statement balance; it is marked pending when cleared transfers alone do not cover the balance. Payment timing compares that date with the bank due date and retains the number of days late. A missed minimum after its due date shows days overdue. If the minimum was met but the full balance remains, timing shows **Balance carried** rather than overdue. Debt history retains **Paid late** after a late full payment, and **Minimum paid late** for a carried balance. Estimates and incomplete payment coverage remain explicit.

**Full remaining** and **Minimum status** on Statements compare the bank obligations with those transfers; Overview also shows **Minimum remaining**. A minimum met after its due date is labelled **Met late**. Late transfers reduce remaining amounts while payment timing preserves the delay. Refunds and new spending affect Actual debt separately; refunds are not recorded as repayments or included in Total paid. Uncleared purchases do not hide known repayment totals.

You do not need to allocate a repayment to individual purchases. Statement matching is automatic. Bench retains an internal opening-debt breakdown for analysis, separate from repayment tracking. Payments beyond a statement obligation are shown separately from remaining card credit. They may have funded later activity, so only the signed Actual account surplus establishes current credit. Pending payments remain explicit. Rolling statement balances are never added together as new principal.

## Understand debt and history

The card list shows debt, limit, available credit, utilization, due date, minimum remaining, payment status, purchase APR and card status. Account names appear as secondary text only when they differ from the card nickname. Portfolio totals report missing coverage. **Statement payments remaining** uses each card’s latest confirmed billed obligation once, deducts confirmed payments and shows minimum, overdue and pending amounts separately. APR remains in the table and settings.

**Debt & history** focuses on actual history: debt carried at each due date, billed balances with growth drivers, and cumulative identified interest and fees. Actual carried debt appears only after the due date has passed, uses confirmed payments by that date and stays unchanged by later repayment. Upcoming statements are omitted. Missing coverage and pending deadline evidence remain unknown rather than zero. Historical charts connect consecutive known statement observations with straight lines and retain observation markers. These lines show a trend, not reconstructed daily balances; missing periods interrupt the line. Filled markers identify confirmed bank values or observed Actual evidence; hollow markers identify totals including estimates. Source labels in tooltips explain which evidence supports each point. Late-payment outcomes remain separate. A known-zero interest chart is collapsed until requested; missing classification is explained without claiming zero charges.

All trend charts use the loan calculator’s interactive Recharts library and visual conventions, with theme colors, dashed grids, exact-value tooltips and keyboard point navigation. Focus a chart and use the arrow keys to inspect statements. Charts respect reduced motion. **View trend values** opens a dialog; **Review spending, refunds and interest classification** shows the source table using a labelled button.

Select the card's Actual interest/fee categories in Settings to identify those charges automatically, or classify individual rows with a reason. Without category mappings or complete current individual classification, interest, fees and new-spending breakdowns remain unavailable rather than claiming zero charges or treating all outflows as spending. **Set charge categories** opens settings directly. Portfolio totals disclose how many cards have classification coverage. Merchant names are not proof of an interest charge. **Interest charged** uses Actual posting dates and shows the available reporting period. It includes only loaded activity within that period, including history crossing a calendar year; it does not fetch earlier transactions to manufacture an annual total. Interest charged is not claimed to be interest paid, and transfers alone cannot establish the bank's interest/principal allocation or lifetime totals.

**Interest illustration** defaults to **Daily simple Actual/365** for new cards and uses the same engine as Loans & Debt. Supported daily simple 365/360-day profiles assume purchase APR on all current debt, monthly capitalization, no grace period, no new spending and no repayments for 30 days. The first capitalization occurs at the next statement closing date after the illustration starts; interest then accrues on the increased balance. Unsupported combinations show an unavailable result. Bank-posted charges stay separate; Bench never posts the estimate into Actual. Choose Off when these assumptions do not fit the card; an existing explicit Off setting is preserved.

Saved actions show feedback on the right of the tabs, below Settings. **Undo** reverses recent workspace changes; close an editor or review first. A source refresh preserves unsaved editors and review decisions.

## Current testing phase

This implementation keeps all credit-card records, decisions and undo history in tab memory. They survive client navigation and budget switching, with separate working sets per budget. Reloading clears them. No credit-card DB layer, migrations or browser-storage persistence have been introduced. Persistence will follow the reviewed testing phase.

### Balance movement and minimum-payment cost

All workbench dates use **DD MMM YYYY**, including statement periods, payment details, date inputs, chart axes/tooltips and payoff dates. Search accepts the displayed format as well as ISO dates; sorting and calculations still use stored ISO dates. Source transaction notes and `#YYYY-MM` tags retain their original text.

The statement list uses eight columns with vertically centred cells and at most three lines per statement. **Statement date** shows the closing date and a readable period. **Statement balance** shows the closing balance with **Minimum due** beneath it, clearly marking estimates; **Due date** follows immediately. **Balance movement** has three labelled lines: previous balance and purchases, interest and fees, then signed **cycle payments** and refunds. Cycle payments explain how the closing balance was reached; **Payment progress** describes repayments attributed to that statement afterward. Other adjustments and a nonzero difference remain visible in the movement summary; select a line for the complete breakdown. Missing preceding history stays explicit, and unknown amounts explain what needs review rather than inventing zeros.

**Payment progress** shows the recorded total paid, minimum met or remaining, and full settlement or remaining balance. Pending amounts are identified, and remaining figures that include them are provisional. Overpayments retain the recorded total with an explicit amount beyond the obligation; this is distinct from unused account credit. **Payment timing** independently shows when cumulative payments reached the minimum and full balance, with on-time or days-late outcomes. Dates use **DD MMM YYYY** (for example, **24 Oct 2026**); select timing text for its full explanation. Pending transfers never imply confirmed settlement: a recorded threshold date is marked pending until confirmed evidence covers it. For example, the minimum can be on time while the full payment is three days late. A met minimum with a remaining full balance is labelled **Balance carried**, separately from a missed minimum. Zero/credit-balance statements say **No payment required**. **Status** describes statement evidence independently from payment success, and outlined **Edit** / **Review** actions remain side by side.

Payment progress and timing remain independent of spending verification. Observed interest still requires clearance. Search, filters and sorting remain available.

Transaction review keeps each transaction on one line. Long payees, notes and categories show an ellipsis; select the text or focus and press Enter to read the complete source in a popover. Full text remains searchable. The decision buttons show the selected choice with an icon, border and pressed state; the classification label itself opens its editor, and reasons remain directly editable. Payment notes use the available column width instead of a fixed text-width cap. Transaction review includes Actual categories and the same classification controls as Debt & history. Classify opens a small editor with a required reason and saves only the workspace classification; membership decisions remain draft until the statement save action. Include, Exclude and Undecided buttons support keyboard activation and are also available for bulk selection. Undecided is the unresolved review state. Save decisions appears when verification is blocked; Save & verify commits eligible decisions together. Opening evidence uses Confirm transactions. Negative review amounts are red, and incoming transfer payments are green; positive refunds keep their ordinary amount styling. Signs and classification labels remain visible.

Charts are compact and use three columns on wide screens. Balance tooltips identify spending, interest, fees, refunds and cycle payments separately. The cumulative-interest chart can show a hypothetical zero-interest baseline; this is not a reconstruction of a past grace period or interest paid. Upcoming obligations use **Upcoming**, **Due soon** (within seven days), **Due today** or **Overdue**, with payment outcomes kept separate.

The collapsed **What if I only pay the minimum?** section opens optional **Cost of minimum payments** modelling. It uses the Loans & Debt engine from current Actual debt. It keeps the configured minimum percentage/floor and APR unchanged, with no new spending or fees, daily simple accrual and monthly capitalization. Payments start at the next projected cycle due date; every later due date is independently recalculated from its billing cycle, preserving the configured day after February and other short months. Bank-confirmed exceptions apply to saved statements, not hypothetical future cycles. Interest is not capped by the credit limit. It shows projected balance including accrued interest, cumulative future interest and a payoff date only after both posted debt and exact accrued interest are settled under the engine’s rounding policy. Small residual interest may require another posting/payment cycle. Chart tooltips distinguish posted from accrued interest; summaries and charts end on the same payoff date or 30-year horizon. Warnings distinguish minimum payments that do not cover accrued interest from debt repayment too slow to finish within the horizon. Otherwise it reports **Not repaid within 30 years** and interest over that bounded horizon, rather than a lifetime total. An unavailable configuration or unsupported engine outcome is reported explicitly. **Future interest if paid in full now** is a hypothetical zero-future-interest benchmark, with the actual payoff amount stated separately; accrued/trailing charges and issuer grace-period rules are not inferred. Separate purchase, cash-advance and balance-transfer APR buckets, loss/restoration of grace and issuer-specific residual-interest rules are not modeled. Monthly capitalization remains an explicit illustration assumption, not a claim about your issuer. These scenarios never change Actual transactions or recorded bank balances.

A transaction saved as included in one statement appears automatically excluded and locked in other statement reviews, with a reason such as **Included in statement 30 Sep 2026**. Bulk decisions cannot include it elsewhere. To reassign it, open its owning review and choose **Move** or **Move selected**. Select a confirmed destination and stage the move. Saving excludes it from the source and includes it in the destination atomically; the destination returns to review. Closing without saving discards moves. Stale revisions, changed source evidence, incomplete coverage and other-card targets are rejected. You can also exclude and save before including elsewhere. Changing source transaction details does not release this ownership automatically.


## Net activity since statement

Overview’s **Net activity since statement** compares the current outstanding amount with the latest billed balance. Its bridge groups posting-date transactions after closing into spending, payments, refunds, interest, fees and other adjustments, with pending items and any unexplained difference explicit. Open **View contributing transactions** to inspect the entries. Scheduled future transactions are excluded from this bridge. The difference is not automatically called new purchases.


## Choose the first tracked statement

In card Settings, set **First tracked statement** to the closing date of the first cycle you want Bench to track. Leaving it blank initially uses the first saved statement. That specific confirmed statement establishes the opening anchor; earlier cycles are not automatically drafted or saved. Each card has its own boundary. Existing opening statements supply the boundary for older session records.

The 6/12/24/36-month toolbar range controls routine activity loading and automatic previews, independently of the tracking start. Known saved statements remain accessible. Reads stop at the first tracked cycle’s start, with a two-day margin needed to review posting cutoffs. Opening a saved statement review explicitly loads its older tracked period when necessary; it never adds earlier tracked statements or silently moves the anchor. Exact bank-entered period dates override the recurring period estimate for the first statement. Interest and payment analysis remain unavailable when the required tracked history is outside the loaded range.

Changing the start reopens saved statements for review and recalculates the opening anchor. Move it earlier to record an earlier opening cycle; Bench does not invent its bank balance. Moving it forward requires deleting saved statements before the new start first, so changing Settings cannot silently discard bank facts. If no saved confirmed statement matches the chosen date, the opening anchor remains missing.

## Reset or delete a saved statement

Open the **Statement actions** menu beside Edit and Review in the Statements table. Both actions ask for confirmation and support the workspace’s **Undo**.

- **Reset transaction review** removes the statement’s saved inclusion/exclusion/review decisions and bank-posting overrides, clears verification, and regenerates suggestions next time you review. It releases transaction ownership while preserving bank values and separately saved activity classifications. Dependent later statements and saved reviews referencing released transactions reopen for review.
- **Delete saved statement** removes its saved bank values, review decisions, correction records and allocations referencing its opening origin. It releases transaction ownership and reopens dependent statements. Actual Budget transactions and activity classifications are untouched. An unsaved automatic estimate may reappear; deletion removes the saved record, not the underlying billing cycle.

Deleting the opening statement preserves the configured tracking date. Later statements are not silently promoted to an opening anchor: restore the missing statement, use Undo, or explicitly change the tracking start in Settings. Revision guards reject actions from stale dialogs, and Undo restores the records and decisions without making older editor revisions valid again.


## All cards dashboard

The dashboard supports one card or many. Monthly statement debt uses month labels rather than inventing a closing date on the first of each month. Tooltips identify each contributing card, its actual closing date and balance; a month missing any registered card remains a gap. Confirmed values and estimates stay distinguished.

**Interest charged by month** appears whenever loaded Actual activity contains identified, cleared interest, even for a single reporting month. It displays identified monthly charges across the available card histories. Different tracking windows, unavailable cards and incomplete classifications do not hide known charges: tooltips label partial coverage, show each card’s exact reporting dates, and leave unknown contributions unavailable. Partial totals include identified charges only, not an estimate of missing interest. Months without any usable evidence remain gaps. The chart is hidden when no interest charges have been identified, leaving the debt chart the available width. The top Interest charged KPI retains each card’s available reporting period and coverage.

Payment status shows amount outcomes separately from timing: Upcoming, Due soon (within seven days), Due today, the number of days past the full-payment deadline, or the historical days late after settlement. A minimum met remains a minimum met even when the full balance is carried. The payment KPI shows nonzero minimum, overdue-minimum and pending amounts, flags partial coverage, and otherwise reports no minimum outstanding. Hover its amount for the confirmed-card count. Currency display is unchanged.

The generic explanation of chart lines, gaps and markers is omitted beneath charts. Source details remain available in the interactive tooltips.
