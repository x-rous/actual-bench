Accounts is where you manage your accounts in bulk, and where you tell Bench what each account
*is*: a checking account, savings, a credit card, a house, a loan. That one label, the **class**,
lets Financial Insights, Cashflow Planning, Credit Cards and Assets read your money correctly.

Open it from **Budget Setup → Accounts**. Account edits are staged and go to Actual when you select
**Save**; classes and access settings are Bench's own and save straight away.

![The Accounts table grouped by account group: credit cards, daily banking, investments, loans, and property and vehicles, each account with its group, class, whether its money is accessible, balance, budget placement, status and rule references, and a total.](../../../assets/screenshots/user-guide/accounts.png)

*Each account's class and whether its money counts as cash sit beside its balance, budget placement
and rule references, grouped the way you group them in Actual.*

## What you can do here

- Create accounts one at a time, or paste dozens from a spreadsheet, each with a starting balance.
- Close, reopen and delete several accounts at once, with the balance, transactions and rules in
  front of you first.
- Give each account a class, or set one on a whole account group.
- Group the table by account group or by class, with subtotals.
- Pull new transactions from your banks with **Sync banks**.

Everything here uses the shared [editing model](../../getting-started/editing-and-saving/).

## Create an account

1. Select **Add Account**.
2. Enter a **Name** and, if you like, an **Initial Balance**.
3. Choose **on-budget** or **off-budget**. Actual does not let you change this later, so choose
   carefully.
4. Optionally choose an **Account Group** and an **Account Class** (see below).
5. Select **Add Account** to stage it, then **Save** to create it in Actual.

To create several at once, use **+ Add row** at the bottom of the table, or paste rows from a
spreadsheet. The **Balance** column is editable on new rows.

## Close, reopen and delete

- Closing or deleting always confirms first, showing the balance, transaction count and rule count.
- Closing an account that still holds money offers **Move the balance to** another open account, as
  Actual does. Moving money from an on-budget account to an off-budget one needs a category. Leave it
  empty to close the account with its balance where it is.
- Balances refresh about once a minute, or when you select **Refresh**. Negative balances show in
  red.

## Account classes

A class tells Bench what kind of account this is. It is a label in Bench only: it never changes
anything in Actual, and it is separate from the type Actual keeps for bank sync.

| Heading | Classes | Budget placement |
|---|---|---|
| Everyday money | **Checking** (current, everyday and offset accounts), **Cash** | On or off budget |
| Savings | **Savings** (high-yield, money market, notice accounts) | On or off budget |
| | **Fixed Deposit** (term deposits, CDs, pledged deposits) | Off budget |
| Credit | **Credit Card** | On budget, so purchases count as spending |
| Investments | **Investment**, **Retirement** (pensions, superannuation) | Off budget |
| Things you own | **Property**, **Vehicle**, **Other Fixed Asset** (gold, collectibles, equipment) | Off budget |
| | **Other Asset** (money owed to you, rental bonds, prepaid balances) | On or off budget |
| Debts | **Loan** (mortgages, personal, auto and student loans, lines of credit) | Off budget |
| | **Other Liability** | On or off budget |

To set one, click an account's **Class** cell and choose. A class that needs the other placement is
shown greyed out, so you cannot pick one that does not fit. To classify several accounts, select
them and choose **Set class…**. The **Class** filter, including **Unclassified**, finds the accounts
still waiting for one.

The **Guide** button at the top of the page explains each class, why it matters, and the quickest
way to get every account ready.

### Classes on account groups

In **Manage Groups** you can give a whole account group a class. Its accounts then show it as
**Inherited**, and you change it on the group. If some accounts already have a class of their own,
Bench lists them and asks you to confirm. A narrower class is kept: Savings or Fixed Deposit inside a
Checking group, Savings inside a Cash group, or Other Fixed Asset inside an Other Asset group still
applies.

If an account's own class is overridden by its group, a notice at the top of the table names it.
**Keep the group's class** clears the account's own class; **Dismiss** hides the notice.

## What counts as cash

<span id="accessible"></span><span id="reports"></span>

Two more columns decide how Financial Insights and Cashflow Planning treat each account. You don't
need to touch them: every class has a sensible default.

**Accessible** answers "could I use this money now if I needed it?"

- On-budget accounts are always **Available (on budget)**.
- Off-budget Checking, Cash and Savings default to **Available now**. Fixed Deposit, Investment,
  Retirement and Other Asset default to **Locked**. Change it for, say, a notice account you cannot
  touch, or a no-penalty CD you can.
- Credit cards, things you own and debts have no access setting.

Available Checking and Cash count as cash, and available Savings join them when Financial Insights is
set to **Cash + savings**. An available Investment, Retirement or Other Asset account is money you
could reach: it shows in the cash buffer's second figure, never its headline. Locked money counts
towards your net worth, never your cash. [Financial Insights](../financial-insights/) explains each
figure.

**Reports** lets you leave an off-budget account out of every figure in Financial Insights and out of
Cashflow's automatic rows. A left-out account needs no class. The **left out of reports** shortcut
beside the class filter lists them. For a group whose accounts are all off budget, the same setting
is in **Manage Groups**.

To change several at once, select them and choose **Set access…**: **Available now**, **Locked**,
**Leave out of reports** or **Include in reports**. Bench tells you how many it could not change and
why.

Classes, access and reports settings are saved in Bench for this budget straight away, without
**Save**, and are not part of undo. On a new account they wait with its draft, and are saved once
Actual has created it.

## Grouped view

Use the **No grouping / By group / By class** toggle to cluster the table. Each heading shows how
many accounts it holds and their total balance, and a **Total** row closes the table. Select a
heading to collapse it; collapsed rows are left out of select-all and paste, so a bulk action never
touches rows you cannot see. Debts are negative, so a heading with loans or cards can total below
zero.

## Account groups

Account groups are the named groups Actual shows in its sidebar. They need Actual 26.9 or later (in
HTTP API mode, an `actual-http-api` release for 26.9 or later).

- Click an account's **Group** cell to choose a group, **No group** or **New group…**.
- Select several accounts and choose **Move to group…** to move them together.
- **Manage Groups** creates, renames and deletes groups. Deleting a group keeps its accounts; they
  become ungrouped.

Group changes are staged like any other account edit. To reorder groups, use Actual itself.

## Sync banks

**Sync banks** asks Actual to pull new transactions from the banks you linked in Actual (through
SimpleFIN or GoCardless, for example), right now. It only appears when your connection can start a
bank sync. To run it on a schedule instead, see [Bank Sync](../bank-sync/).

## Notes, filters and CSV

- **Notes:** every row has a note button. Notes render as Markdown and save as soon as you confirm
  them.
- **Filter and sort** by name, status, budget placement, group, class and whether any rules use the
  account.
- **CSV columns:** `name` (required), `offBudget`, `closed`, and `group` where groups are supported.
  A group name that does not exist yet is created. Exports also include a `class` column; on import
  it is for information only, so set classes in the table.

## Common problems

- **The on-budget / off-budget switch is greyed out:** Actual fixes it when the account is created.
- **A class is greyed out:** it needs the other budget placement (see the table above), or the
  account inherits its class from its group.
- **Financial Insights says an account needs attention:** give it a class that fits its placement,
  or leave it out of reports.
- **A new row stays highlighted:** it is staged. Select **Save**.
